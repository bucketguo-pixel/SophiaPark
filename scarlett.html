Victoria

Transforming artistic thought into modern pieces that can be worn and felt.

EducationHarvard University
FocusDesign · Materials · Development

From concept to couture

A Harvard University graduate, Victoria combines a rigorous grounding in design theory with a global outlook. She leads couture design, creative development, material research and the evolution of the house aesthetic, guiding the team from initial concept and drape to final garment.

Design does not decorate the body; it reveals the wearer’s existing strength through proportion, material and movement.
