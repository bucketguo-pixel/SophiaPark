From Seoul to Los Angeles

Sophia Park graduated from Parsons School of Design in New York in 2009, focusing on fashion design and womenswear. From 2010 to 2017, she developed her experience in styling, creative direction and luxury brand management. In 2018, at age 31, she moved to Los Angeles and co-founded SOPHIA PARK with her close friend Seraphina.

Her discerning eye and commercial instinct have brought sustained industry recognition to her work. Having served as both Design Director and Fashion Marketing Director, Sophia Park now also mentors emerging creative talent in design thinking and strategic planning.

Precision gives form. Freedom gives life.
